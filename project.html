4. Label Guide

SIF Precursor tab maana jata hai jab report me kuch aisa ho jisse jaan ka khatra ho sakta tha:

  • Gas leak, H2S, explosion, fire jaisi cheezein
  • Height par kaam bina harness ke
  • Bhaari load ke neeche kisi ka hona
  • Bina permit ya bina lockout ke kaam
  • Confined space me dikkat
  • "narrowly missed" ya "close call" jaisi baatein

Safe / Routine tab maana jata hai jab report housekeeping, minor ya cosmetic ho, ya khatre ka control maujood ho (permit in place, properly isolated, PPE worn).
